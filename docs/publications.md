# Publications

## 2025

### An Effective Quantification of Methane Point-Source Emissions with the Multi-Level Matched Filter from Hyperspectral Imagery

**Menglei Liang**, Ying Zhang, Liangfu Chen, Jinhua Tao, Meng Fan, and Chao Yu  
*Remote Sensing*, 17(5), 843, 2025.

We introduced the Multi-Level Matched Filter to mitigate the underestimation of strong methane plumes in conventional matched-filter retrievals. The method was evaluated with radiance simulations, synthetic plume experiments, and satellite observations from a controlled methane-release campaign.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs17050843){ .academic-inline-link }

### MPSUNet: A Deep Learning-Based Segmentation Framework for Methane Plume Detection With Space-Based Hyperspectral and Multispectral Imagery

Cheng Chen, Meng Fan, Zhibao Wang, **Menglei Liang**, Jinhua Tao, and Liangfu Chen  
*IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing*, 63, 1–15, 2025.

This work developed a deep-learning framework for detecting and segmenting methane plumes across space-based hyperspectral and multispectral methane products.
