# About

I am a PhD student in Atmospheric Science at the **University at Albany, State University of New York**, and a graduate researcher at the **Atmospheric Sciences Research Center**.

My research focuses on methane remote sensing and scientific machine learning. Before joining UAlbany, I completed an MS at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences.

I am a member of the [Atmospheric Modeling and Remote Sensing Lab](https://yu-xue-ying.github.io/group/).
