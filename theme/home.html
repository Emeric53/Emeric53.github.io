I study methane emissions using satellite remote sensing and scientific machine learning.

Research

Methane plume detection and quantification
Detecting plumes, retrieving concentration enhancements, and estimating point-source emission rates from hyperspectral satellite observations.
Scientific machine learning
Data-driven retrieval and plume-segmentation methods that remain interpretable and testable against physical expectations.
Atmospheric transport and multiscale monitoring
Connecting satellite observations with transport models, from individual facilities to broader spatial scales.
