# Research

I work at the intersection of atmospheric science, satellite remote sensing, and machine learning. My research turns satellite observations into reliable information about greenhouse-gas emissions.

## Research directions

### Methane point-source detection and quantification

Detecting methane plumes, retrieving concentration enhancements, and estimating point-source emission rates from hyperspectral satellite observations.

### Scientific machine learning

Developing data-driven retrieval and plume-segmentation methods that remain interpretable and testable against physical expectations.

### Atmospheric transport and multiscale monitoring

Connecting satellite observations with transport models to study emissions from individual facilities to broader spatial scales.

## Selected method

### Multi-Level Matched Filter

The Multi-Level Matched Filter uses a radiance look-up table and piecewise regression to reduce high-concentration underestimation in conventional matched-filter retrievals. It was evaluated with radiance simulations, synthetic plumes, and controlled-release observations.
